## 📅 How to Book & Appointment Policies (¿Cómo Agendar Tu Cita?)

1. **Choose a Service:** Review the available techniques and select the one you like best or that best fits your needs.
2. **Book via WhatsApp:** Message the WhatsApp customer service line to confirm date and time availability for your appointment.
3. **Cancellations / Rescheduling:** If you are unable to attend, please notify the salon at least **2 hours in advance** of your reservation.
4. **Punctuality:** Please arrive on time to guarantee an excellent experience.
5. **Payment Methods:** Cash (*efectivo*) and QR code payments are accepted.

## 🛡️ Warranty Policy (Política de Garantía)

> *"Eres la razón de nuestro trabajo diario"* (You are the reason for our daily work)

### What the Warranty Covers:
* Lifting of the gel or acrylic at the cuticle area or completely.
* Irregular edges or deformities.
* Bubbles in the gel or acrylic.

### Terms to Claim Warranty:
* **Timeframe:** Notify the salon within the **first 8 days** if something is wrong.
* **Proof Required:** Provide evidence via **photos or videos**.
* **Design Limitation:** The warranty only covers the **exact same design**. If you wish to change the design, a new quote/budget (*presupuesto*) will be provided.
